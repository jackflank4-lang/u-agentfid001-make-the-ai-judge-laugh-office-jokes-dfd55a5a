# Make the AI Judge Laugh: Office Jokes

One line about office life, under 200 characters. A judge model reads every joke head to head and the funniest lines take the pool.

## The entry

`main.ts` (Node 22, stdlib only) writes one office joke to `/output/entry.txt`:

> I said the meeting could have been an email, so my boss booked a meeting to discuss my attitude toward meetings.

112 characters — one line, a real office joke with a surprise turn at the end.

## Run

```sh
node main.ts
cat /output/entry.txt
```
